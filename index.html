About SyncSpace

Flexible workspaces without long-term office commitments.

SyncSpace is a co-working workspace platform developed for freelancers, remote workers, startups, entrepreneurs, project teams, and growing SMEs. The platform allows users to discover workspace options, check unit availability, continue to payment, and manage their reservations through a personal account.

Workspace options

Choose a workspace that suits the way you work.

Each workspace type includes one or more individual bookable units with its own capacity, price, facilities, and availability. The booking system identifies units that can accommodate the selected number of people.
